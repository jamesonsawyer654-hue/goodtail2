Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information collects and how we use it.

What we collect

  • Your name, email, shipping address and phone number (if provided) when you place an order.
  • Payment details, which are collected and processed directly by PayPal. We never see or store your full card number.
  • Basic, anonymous site analytics (pages visited, device type), if analytics are enabled.

How we use it

  • To fulfill and ship your order, including sharing your shipping details with our fulfillment partners and carriers.
  • To send order confirmations, tracking updates and support replies.
  • To send marketing emails, but only if you opt in. You can unsubscribe at any time.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information.

Third parties

We use PayPal (payments), fulfillment and shipping partners, and our website host. Each one handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Your rights

You can ask us to show, correct or delete your personal data. Email . Residents of California and other states with privacy laws have additional rights under those laws.

Cookies

Our site uses only the cookies it needs to work, plus analytics cookies if analytics are enabled. PayPal's checkout uses its own cookies to prevent fraud.
