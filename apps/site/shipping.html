Shipping Policy

Cost

Shipping is free on all orders.

Processing time

Orders are processed within 1–3 business days after payment. You'll receive an email with your tracking number once your order ships.

Delivery time

Most orders arrive in after shipping. Delivery times are estimates and may be longer during holidays or carrier delays. If your order is delayed, we'll email you with an update and give you the option to cancel for a full refund.

Where we ship

We currently ship to addresses in the United States.

Multiple packages

Items in the same order may ship from different warehouses and arrive in separate packages.

Lost or damaged packages

If tracking shows no movement for 10 business days, or your item arrives damaged, email with your order number and a photo (if damaged). We'll send a replacement or a full refund.

Wrong address

Please double-check your address at checkout. If you spot a mistake, email us within 12 hours and we'll fix it before the order ships.
