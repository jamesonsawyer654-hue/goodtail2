Returns & Refunds

We want your pet to love their new gear. If something isn't right, we'll fix it.

-day guarantee

You can request a return within days of delivery. Email with your order number and the reason for the return.

Damaged, defective or wrong item

Send us a photo and we'll ship a free replacement or issue a full refund. You won't need to send the item back.

Changed your mind

Items must be unused and in their original packaging. We'll email you the return address. Return shipping is paid by the customer. Once we receive the item, we'll refund you to your original payment method within 5 business days.

Non-returnable items

For hygiene reasons, items that have been used on or by a pet (such as brushes or lick mats) can't be returned for a change-of-mind refund. They're still covered if they're defective.

Cancellations

You can cancel for a full refund any time before your order ships. Email us as soon as possible.

Refund timing

After a refund is issued, it can take 5–10 business days for your bank to show it.
