# Good Tail Co. — 30-Day Content Plan (every post written for you)

**Your whole job:** film the shots listed (5–20 seconds each, on your phone), paste the caption, post.
Every video goes to **TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts**. Same video, same caption, three platforms.
**Post 2** each day = the same clips re-cut with the alternate hook. Two posts a day doubles your chances of getting views.

**Batch it:** you don't have to post live every day. Film the whole week in one hour, then schedule the posts for free:
- TikTok: desktop web → Upload → **Schedule** (up to 10 days ahead)
- Instagram: **Meta Business Suite** → Planner (free)
- YouTube: Studio → upload → **Schedule**
- Pinterest: create a pin → **Publish later** (up to 30 days ahead)

**Always:** add captions to every video (CapCut → Auto captions), use a trending sound at low volume, and reply to every comment within the first hour.

## Pinterest: 3 pins a day (10 minutes, schedule a week at once)

Pins send traffic for **months**, so this is the closest thing to "set and forget" advertising.
1. Create a free **Pinterest Business** account (it's free and gives you analytics).
2. Create boards: *Dog Grooming Tips*, *Pet Hair Hacks*, *Dog Walk Essentials*, *Calm Dog Ideas*.

## Paid ads: only with profit, never with the starting $50

Wait until **one organic video** gets 10k+ views *and* brings sales. Then:
- **TikTok:** Promote that exact video (Spark Ads / "Promote" button). Start at $10/day for 3 days. Goal: Website visits → later Purchases.
- **Instagram:** Boost that Reel, $10/day for 3 days. Audience: US, 22–55, interests *dogs, pet grooming, pet supplies*.
- **Stop rule:** if one sale costs more than **$8** in ad spend (half the kit profit), pause and test a different video.
- **Ad copy to use:**
  1. "Shedding season? This brush cleans itself in one click. Free US shipping + 30-day guarantee."
  2. "Stop buying sticky lint sheets. One reusable roller, couch fur gone."
  3. "The 3-piece Shed-Free Home Kit: brush, hair remover and lick mats. Save $12.98."
